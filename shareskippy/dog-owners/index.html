How it works for dog owners

  1. 01

    Create your dog's profile

    Sign up free and add your dog — photo, breed, energy level, and what kind of help you need. Takes about 5 minutes.

  2. 02

    Review and approve connections

    Dog lovers in your area will send connection requests. Read their profiles, check community reviews, and approve only the people you're comfortable with.

  3. 03

    Coordinate on your terms

    Message directly, set the schedule, share care instructions. You stay fully in control of when, how often, and for how long.

Built for owner peace of mind

You approve every person. Your address stays private. There's always a way to reach someone.

You approve every connection

No one interacts with your dog without your explicit OK. Review profiles and community reviews before saying yes.

Your address stays private

Only approximate neighborhood info is shown publicly. You share your actual location only with people you've already approved.

Emergency contacts, always

Every connection includes emergency contact info shared between both parties, so there's always a line of communication.
