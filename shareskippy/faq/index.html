General

Yes — always. ShareSkippy is a nonprofit community platform run by ShareVita. There are no fees, no subscriptions, and no charges between members. Dog sharing is powered by community goodwill, not transactions.

ShareSkippy is active in 50+ cities across the US and growing. When you sign up, you'll see dogs and dog lovers in your area. If your city is small, you might be one of the first — and that's a great thing.

Yes. Creating a free account lets you connect with neighbors, post your dog, or browse dogs near you. Signup takes about two minutes — no credit card needed.

ShareSkippy connects dog owners who need help with dog lovers who want to spend time with dogs — for free. Think of it as a neighborhood network built around dogs and the people who love them.

ShareSkippy works in your browser on any device — phone, tablet, or desktop. No download required. Just head to shareskippy.com to get started.

ShareSkippy is a project of ShareVita, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit based in Oakland, CA. Our mission is to build community through sharing — and we believe dogs are one of the best ways to do that.

For Dog Owners

Sign up at shareskippy.com and create a profile for your dog — photo, name, breed, energy level, and what kind of help you're looking for (walks, playdates, etc.). It takes about 5 minutes.

Absolutely. You approve every connection. No one interacts with your dog without your explicit OK. You can review profiles, read community reviews, and message someone before agreeing to anything.

Every interaction includes emergency contact information shared between both parties. Our community guidelines set clear expectations, and all members are subject to community reviews. For issues, reach us at team@sharevita.org.

No. ShareSkippy uses approximate location — never your exact address. You coordinate meeting details privately with people you've already chosen to connect with.

You can include all of that in your dog's profile — medications, behavioral notes, leash preferences, foods to avoid. You control what's shared and who sees it.

Nothing mandatory. You decide the frequency, duration, and who you connect with. Listing your dog just signals that you're open to help — no connection happens until you approve it.

For Dog Lovers

Sign up at shareskippy.com, then browse the community to see dogs in your area. When you find a pup you'd love to spend time with, send a connection request to the owner. They'll review your profile and respond.

No — ShareSkippy is a volunteer community platform. The reward is quality time with a dog, the mental health benefits that come with it, and genuinely helping a neighbor.

Show up when you say you will, follow the owner's care instructions, keep the dog safe, and communicate openly. Most importantly: be reliable. This community runs on trust.

You're expected to take reasonable care of the dog. Specific arrangements — including liability — are between you and the owner. We recommend discussing expectations clearly before your first outing.

Yes, and many connections do become ongoing. Dog owners and dog lovers often form weekly routines — the dog gets a reliable companion, and you get a standing date you actually look forward to.

Safety

All members create public profiles. Reviews and ratings accumulate over time, building a reputation system that helps everyone make informed decisions before connecting with someone.

Yes. We don't share your exact location or contact details publicly. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

ShareSkippy's community guidelines set expectations for safe, respectful interactions. All members agree to these when they sign up. They're available to read on shareskippy.com after creating your account.
