The Basics

General Questions

Is ShareSkippy really free?

Yes — always. ShareSkippy is a nonprofit community platform run by ShareVita. There are no fees, no subscriptions, and no hidden charges. Dog sharing is powered by community goodwill, not transactions.

What cities is ShareSkippy available in?

ShareSkippy is active in 50+ cities across the US and growing. When you sign up, you'll see dogs and dog lovers in your area. If your city is small, you might be one of the first — and that's a great thing.

Do I need to create an account?

Yes. Creating a free account lets you connect with people in your neighborhood, post your dog, or browse dogs near you. Signup takes about two minutes — no credit card needed.

What is ShareSkippy, exactly?

ShareSkippy is a community platform that connects dog owners who need help with dog lovers who want to spend time with dogs — for free. Think of it as a neighborhood network for dogs and the people who love them.

Is ShareSkippy an app?

ShareSkippy works in your browser on any device — phone, tablet, or desktop. You don't need to download anything to get started at shareskippy.com.

Who runs ShareSkippy?

ShareSkippy is a project of ShareVita, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit based in Oakland, CA. Our mission is to build community through sharing. We're a small team driven by a big belief: neighbors helping neighbors changes lives.

For Dog Owners

Dog Owner Questions

How do I list my dog on ShareSkippy?

Sign up at shareskippy.com, then create a profile for your dog — photo, name, breed, energy level, and what kind of help you're looking for (walks, playdates, etc.). It takes about 5 minutes.

Can I choose who walks my dog?

Absolutely. You approve every connection. No one interacts with your dog without your explicit OK. You can review profiles, read community reviews, and message people before agreeing to anything.

What if something goes wrong during a walk?

Every interaction includes emergency contact information shared between both parties. Our community guidelines also set clear expectations, and all members are subject to community reviews. If an issue arises, contact our team at team@sharevita.org.

Is my home address shared?

No. ShareSkippy uses approximate location — never your exact address. You coordinate meeting details privately with people you've chosen to connect with.

What if my dog has special needs?

You can include all of that in your dog's profile — medications, behavioral notes, leash preferences, foods to avoid. You're always in control of what's shared and who you connect with.

What does "sharing" my dog actually mean?

It means allowing a vetted community member to take your dog for a walk, hike, or playdate — on your terms, at your schedule. You decide the frequency, duration, and who's involved. Nothing is mandatory.

For Dog Lovers

Dog Lover Questions

How do I find dogs to walk near me?

Sign up at shareskippy.com, then browse the community to see dogs in your area. When you find a pup you'd love to spend time with, send a connection request to the owner. They'll review your profile and respond.

Do I get paid to walk dogs?

No — ShareSkippy is a volunteer community platform. The "payment" is quality time with a dog, the mental health benefits that come with it, and the satisfaction of genuinely helping a neighbor and their pup.

What's expected of me as a dog lover?

Show up when you say you will, follow the owner's care instructions, keep the dog safe, and communicate openly. Most importantly: be kind. This community runs on trust.

What if I'm allergic but still want to participate?

You can filter by breed or coat type when browsing. Some people with mild allergies do fine with certain breeds. Use your best judgment and talk to the dog owner about your situation.

Am I responsible if the dog gets hurt?

You're expected to take reasonable care of the dog. Specific arrangements — including liability — are between you and the dog owner. We recommend agreeing on expectations before your first outing.

Can I borrow dogs regularly, or just once?

Many ShareSkippy connections turn into ongoing weekly relationships — dog owners and dog lovers become genuine friends, and dogs get a reliable companion. It's completely up to you and the owner.

Safety

Safety & Trust

How does ShareSkippy verify members?

All members create profiles that are visible to the community. Reviews and ratings build over time, creating a reputation system that helps everyone make informed decisions about who they connect with.

Is my personal information safe?

Yes. We don't share your exact location, personal contact details, or payment information (there's no payment to begin with). See our Privacy Policy for full details.

What are the community guidelines?

ShareSkippy's community guidelines set expectations for respectful, safe interactions. All members agree to these when signing up. You can review them at shareskippy.com after creating your account.
