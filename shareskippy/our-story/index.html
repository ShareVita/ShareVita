How ShareSkippy Started

Hi, I'm Kaia, the founder of ShareSkippy — a free, community-driven way to connect dog owners with dog lovers for walks, hikes, cuddles, and adventures.

The idea came from a simple act of kindness. A close friend of mine was very sick and couldn't take her dog out for more than a few quick bathroom breaks. I posted on a local Oakland Facebook group asking if anyone could help. Within hours, over 40 people replied. Total strangers. All offering to walk a dog they'd never met.

That moment made me think: there's something profound and deeply human about this — and how dogs can bring people together faster than almost anything else.

Building a Community, Not a Service

I didn't want to build "the next Rover" or "another app." I wanted to create a space that feels neighborly — where people help each other because they want to, not because they're being paid.

There are already plenty of platforms that make dog care transactional. ShareSkippy is about connection over commerce. It's for the busy dog owner who needs help during the day. It's for the student who misses their dog back home. It's for the retiree who just wants some furry company on walks, and everyone in between.

  • 🐕 Dogs get more love and exercise.
  • 🤝 Humans get more connection and purpose.
  • ✨ Everyone wins.

Teaching Myself to Code

When I started, I had zero coding experience. I was coming out of a stressful job in business and operations strategy and needed to build something that felt good.

So I taught myself to code — with a lot of late nights, a lot of trial and error, and a little help from AI tools. The result is what you see here: ShareSkippy.com, built from scratch, designed to be simple, kind, and safe.

Honestly, I'm still learning and always open to feedback or guidance. Feel free to reach me at kaia@sharevita.org.

What Makes ShareSkippy Different

🆓 Free for everyone

There's no payment or membership required — kindness shouldn't cost money.

🏘️ Neighbors helping neighbors

Every connection is community-powered, not transactional.

❤️ Community care

ShareSkippy is part of ShareVita, a nonprofit — so time spent helping a neighbor with their dog is time genuinely well spent.

🔍 Safety through transparency

Public profiles and community reviews help everyone get to know each other before meeting in person.

About Me
