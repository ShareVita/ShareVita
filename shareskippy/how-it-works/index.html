How ShareSkippy Works

Two kinds of people, one community. Dog owners who need help, and dog lovers who want to give it. ShareSkippy brings them together — for free, in your neighborhood.

For Dog Owners

Get Free Help for Your Dog

Life gets busy. ShareSkippy connects you with trusted neighbors who genuinely want to spend time with your dog — on your terms, at your schedule, at zero cost.

1

Create a free account

Sign up at shareskippy.com — no credit card, no subscriptions. Takes about two minutes.

2

Build your dog's profile

Add a photo, breed, energy level, and what kind of help you're looking for — walks, hikes, playdates, or just some company.

3

Set your preferences

Choose your availability and location radius. Review profiles and community ratings before approving any connection.

4

Approve connections you trust

No one interacts with your dog without your explicit OK. You stay in full control — who, when, and how often.

For Dog Lovers

Borrow a Dog Near You

Can't have a dog of your own? You don't need to. ShareSkippy connects you with dog owners in your neighborhood who need exactly your help.

1

Sign up free

Create your profile — tell dog owners a bit about yourself, where you live, and what you're looking for.

2

Browse dogs in your area

See a community map of dogs near you. Browse profiles, read about each pup, and pick one you'd love to walk.

3

Send a connection request

Message the dog owner. They'll review your profile and respond. Most connections happen within a day or two.

4

Meet, walk, and build a bond

Coordinate directly with the owner. Many ShareSkippy connections turn into weekly routines and real friendships.

Built-In Safety

Trust & Safety

✅

Community Verified Profiles

All members build reputation through community reviews over time. You can read what other dog owners and dog lovers say before connecting with anyone.

📍

Privacy-First Locations

Only approximate neighborhood info is shown publicly. Your exact address is never shared — you exchange personal details only with people you choose to connect with.

📞

Emergency Contacts

Every connection includes emergency contact information shared between both parties, so there's always a line of communication if anything comes up.

Quick Answers

Common Questions

Is ShareSkippy really free?

Yes — always. ShareSkippy is a project of ShareVita, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. No fees, subscriptions, or payments between members. Ever.

What cities is it available in?

50+ cities across the US and growing. Sign up to see what's available near you — if your city is small, you could be one of the first.

Do I have to commit to a schedule?

No. As a dog owner you set your own preferences. As a dog lover you arrange things directly with the owner. Nothing is mandatory or automatic.
