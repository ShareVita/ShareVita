How ShareSkippy Works

Two kinds of people, one community. Dog owners who need help, and dog lovers who want to give it. Here's how they find each other.

Join Free Read the FAQ →
For Dog Owners

Get free help for your dog

Life gets busy — work, health, family. ShareSkippy connects you with trusted neighbors who genuinely want to spend time with your pup, on your terms, at no cost.

Post My Dog Free
More for Dog Owners →
  1. 01

    Create a free account

    Sign up at shareskippy.com — no credit card, no subscription. Takes about two minutes.

  2. 02

    Build your dog's profile

    Add a photo, breed, energy level, and what kind of help you're looking for — walks, hikes, playdates, or company.

  3. 03

    Approve connections you trust

    Dog lovers will send connection requests. Review their profiles and community reviews. No one interacts with your dog without your explicit OK.

  4. 04

    Coordinate on your terms

    Message directly, set the schedule, share care instructions. You stay in full control of when, how often, and for how long.

For Dog Lovers

Borrow a dog in your neighborhood

Can't have a dog of your own? You don't need to. ShareSkippy connects you with dog owners in your neighborhood who need exactly your help.

Find Dogs Near Me
More for Dog Lovers →
  1. 01

    Sign up free

    Create your profile — tell dog owners about yourself, your neighborhood, and what you're looking for.

  2. 02

    Browse dogs near you

    See a community map of dogs in your area. Browse profiles, find a pup you'd love to walk, and read about what the owner needs.

  3. 03

    Send a connection request

    Message the owner through ShareSkippy. They'll review your profile and respond — most connections happen within a couple of days.

  4. 04

    Meet, walk, and build a bond

    Coordinate your first outing, follow the owner's care instructions, and enjoy. Many connections turn into weekly routines and genuine friendships.

Safety, built in

Verified profiles, private locations, and emergency contacts — the basics that let both sides trust each other enough to say yes.

Community-verified profiles

All members build reputation through reviews over time. Read what other owners and dog lovers say before connecting with anyone.

Private by default

Only approximate neighborhood info is shown publicly. Your exact address is never shared — you control what personal details go to whom.

Emergency contacts, always

Every connection includes emergency contact information shared between both parties — so there's always a line of communication if anything comes up.

Is ShareSkippy really free?
Yes, always. ShareSkippy is a nonprofit — no fees, subscriptions, or payments between members. Ever.
Do I have to commit to a schedule?
No. Dog owners set their preferences, dog lovers arrange things directly. Nothing is automatic or mandatory.
What cities is it available in?
50+ cities across the US and growing. Sign up to see what's in your area — early members help build the community.
