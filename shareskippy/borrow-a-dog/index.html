The Benefits

Why Borrow a Dog?

❤️

Companionship Without Commitment

Get all the joy, cuddles, and connection of dog ownership — walks, hikes, lazy afternoons — without the full-time responsibility, cost, or lease violations.

🧠

Proven Mental Health Benefits

Time with dogs reduces cortisol, lowers blood pressure, and increases serotonin. Research consistently shows that regular dog interaction is good for you — even when the dog isn't yours.

🌳

Help Dogs Who Need It

Many city dogs don't get enough exercise because their owners are busy or aging. When you borrow a dog, you're genuinely making a difference in that dog's life — and their owner's.

Quick Answers

Common Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, completely. ShareSkippy is a nonprofit community platform — there are no fees, no subscriptions, and no payments between members. You volunteer your time; the reward is the dog.

Am I responsible if something happens?

You're expected to take reasonable care of the dog. Specific expectations are set between you and the dog owner before your first outing. Most owners just want someone kind and reliable.

How do I find dogs near me?

After you sign up at shareskippy.com, you'll see a community map of dogs in your area. Browse profiles, find a pup you like, and send the owner a connection request.
