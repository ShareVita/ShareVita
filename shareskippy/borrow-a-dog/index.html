Why borrow a dog?

You get quality time with a dog. The owner gets relief. The dog gets a walk. It's one of the few things where everyone genuinely wins.

01

Companionship without commitment

Apartment restrictions, allergies, travel — ShareSkippy gives you the joy of dogs without the full-time responsibility of ownership.

02

Real mental health benefits

Time with dogs lowers cortisol, reduces anxiety, and increases serotonin. Regular dog interaction is good for you — even when the dog goes home after.

03

Help dogs who need it

Many city dogs don't get enough exercise. Owners are busy, elderly, or recovering. When you show up for a walk, you're genuinely making a difference.

How it works for dog lovers

You sign up, find dogs near you, send a request, and go for a walk. Most people have their first connection within a week.

Find Dogs Near Me
  1. 01

    Create a free profile

    Sign up at shareskippy.com — free, takes two minutes. Tell dog owners a bit about yourself and what you're looking for.

  2. 02

    Browse dogs in your area

    See a community map of dogs near you. Browse profiles, find a pup you'd love to walk, and send the owner a connection request.

  3. 03

    Meet and build a routine

    Owners review your profile and respond. Coordinate your first outing directly. Many connections become weekly — and real friendships.
